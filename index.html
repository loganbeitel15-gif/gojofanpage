Backstory

Born the strongest.

Light spoiler note: covers Gojo's past (the Hidden Inventory arc) and the early series. Nothing past Shibuya.

Satoru Gojo came into the world as the heir everyone in the Gojo clan had stopped hoping for: the first in roughly four hundred years to be born with both the Six Eyes and the clan's inherited Limitless technique. His birth alone tipped the jujutsu world off balance, and the curses on the other side reportedly grew fiercer in answer. At Tokyo Jujutsu High he was brilliant, cocky and inseparable from his classmate Suguru Geto, with Shoko Ieiri, the healer, completing their trio. Gojo and Geto were called the strongest pair of their generation, and they knew it.

That certainty broke on one mission: escorting Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel. The sorcerer killer Toji Fushiguro, a man with no cursed energy at all, slipped past every defense, cut Gojo down and took Riko's life. Near death, Gojo finally grasped Reverse Cursed Technique, healed himself, and returned to defeat Toji with a power no one could match. He walked away as "the strongest." Geto walked away with doubts that grew until he abandoned the sorcerers' path and defected. Left alone at the top, Gojo chose to teach. He set out to raise a new generation strong enough to stand beside him and to change a rotten system from within, starting with Yuji Itadori, Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki.
