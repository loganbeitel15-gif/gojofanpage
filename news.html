OriginalSpotlight

Character spotlight: Satoru Gojo

In Gege Akutami's Jujutsu Kaisen (published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump), Gojo is a special grade jujutsu sorcerer and a teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He is the heir to the Limitless technique and was born with the Six Eyes, and his Domain Expansion is Unlimited Void. What keeps fans coming back is the contrast: a playful, teasing mentor to his students who is also treated as the strongest sorcerer of his time. Which Gojo moment is your favourite?

Yokohama collaboration: café, merch and holographic stickers

For the anime's 5th anniversary, Yokohama hosted a citywide collaboration through August. It included a themed café at Cafe Fan Base, exclusive acrylic goods and nine holographic mini-character stickers, among them a Gojo, Geto and Shoko design at Yokohama World Porters.
