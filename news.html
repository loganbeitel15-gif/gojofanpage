Season 4 news promised for the Jump Festa 2027 stage

The official anime team says the latest Season 4 (The Culling Game Part 2) information will be shared at its Jump Super Stage on December 19. Cast members Junya Enoki, Mikako Komatsu, Kazuya Nakai and Daisuke Namikawa are scheduled to appear.

Final Exhibition in Shinjuku adds Shinjuku Showdown material

The touring Jujutsu Kaisen exhibition ends at Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Triangle Square from December 25, 2026 to January 25, 2027. This last stop adds never-before-shown storyboards and sketches from Volumes 28–30, Q&A commentary from Gege Akutami, and two random visitor stickers per ticket.

VIZ dates the English print release of Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo

Volume 1 of the sequel manga Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo (story by Gege Akutami, art by Yuji Iwasaki) arrives in U.S. print on January 5, 2027. It comes with a standard cover and three retailer-variant covers.

New Season 4 visual revealed at Juju Fest 2026

A new key visual for The Culling Game Part 2, centred on Sukuna, was shown around Juju Fest 2026 (August 29–30, K-Arena Yokohama), the anime's 5th-anniversary finale event. There is still no release date.

Yokohama collaboration: café, merch and holographic stickers

For the anime's 5th anniversary, Yokohama hosted a citywide collaboration through August. It included a themed café at Cafe Fan Base, exclusive acrylic goods and nine holographic mini-character stickers, among them a Gojo, Geto and Shoko design at Yokohama World Porters.

Season 4: The Culling Game Part 2 teaser revealed

MAPPA's 15th-anniversary lineup livestream debuted the first Season 4 teaser and confirmed the season is in production. Takeru Sato is directing. No release window yet.
